# A Sweep Across The Bay — TFAS Track A redesign

## Source site observations
- Existing site content emphasizes 44+ years of experience, family-owned service, chimney sweeping, repair, caps, water damage, animal removal, fireplace installation and dryer vent cleaning.
- Current pages are content-rich but visually dated and dense. The redesign makes phone/appointment conversion immediate while keeping safety education prominent.

## Design direction
- Warm Florida-home palette: cream base, deep chimney green, ember CTA, gold proof accents.
- Large editorial hero with real existing-site hero image and clear call / request appointment CTAs.
- Service cards use the client’s existing imagery so the preview feels authentic to the business.
- Mobile-first navigation, large tap targets, stacked conversion blocks.

## SEO / preview notes
- Static one-page Vite/React build.
- Noindex/nofollow included for TFAS preview so it does not compete with the live site.
- Title, meta description, Open Graph tags and OG image added.
- No custom domain moved or requested.
